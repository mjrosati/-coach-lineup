Coach Lineup v101 — Center Field Dashboard

Layout:
- PLAYERS upper-left
- STATS lower-left
- FIELD large in the center
- PLAY LINES upper-right
- PLAYS lower-right

Functional previews:
- Players shows roster, position and status.
- Play Lines shows saved lines and active line.
- Stats shows play totals and percentages.
- Plays shows playbook entries.
- Tapping each panel opens its full tool.
- Tapping FIELD opens the full field.
- MAIN DASHBOARD returns to the team dashboard.
- Floating MENU is hidden while this dashboard is open.

No database migration required.
