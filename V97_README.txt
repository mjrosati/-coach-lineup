Coach Lineup v97 — Five Panel Game Day Dashboard

Adds a five-section Game Day dashboard:
1. FIELD — largest section
2. PLAYERS — roster & availability
3. PLAY LINES — current line groups
4. STATS — playing time percentages
5. PLAYS — playbook/calls

Interaction:
- Tap FIELD to expand the football field.
- Tap PLAYERS to open the full roster manager.
- Tap PLAY LINES to open the line manager.
- Tap STATS to open the full stats view.
- Tap PLAYS to open the playbook.
- MAIN DASHBOARD returns to the team dashboard.
- RETURN TO FIELD closes the five-panel view.
- 5-PANEL VIEW button opens this dashboard from Game Day.

Keeps the v96 menu fix, v95 visual redesign, and existing football/game logic.

No database migration required.
