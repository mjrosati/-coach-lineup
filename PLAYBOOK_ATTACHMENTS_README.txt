Coach Lineup — Playbook Attachments

Added:
- Attach one picture or PDF to each play.
- Supported: PDF, JPG/JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC, HEIF.
- Maximum attachment size: 15 MB.
- Existing attachment can be viewed, removed, or replaced.
- Play cards show a Picture/PDF button when an attachment exists.
- Attachments are private to authenticated members of that team.
- Team editors can upload/change/remove attachments.
- Deleting a play also removes its stored attachment.
